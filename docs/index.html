Ask on GitHub ↗

Is the application available in English?+

This website is in English. The application’s interface, built-in prompts and default voices are currently French-first. English interface localization is on the roadmap. The screenshots show the current app.

Do I need an internet connection?+

Online AI providers, cloud transcription and Microsoft voices need internet access. A local setup is possible using Ollama, Whisper and Piper after downloading the models. Local performance depends on your hardware.

Which AI providers can I use?+

The app includes presets for Groq, OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, OpenRouter and Ollama, plus custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints. You choose the provider and model. API pricing, free allowances and quotas depend on the service.

What happens to my manuals and audio?+

PDFs and their search index stay on your machine. When you choose an online AI, the question, supplied conversation history, flight context and relevant manual excerpts are sent to that provider. Online transcription sends audio to the selected service; Whisper can transcribe locally. Online speech synthesis receives the text to read.

Does the overlay work in VR?+

The overlay is not visible in VR or exclusive fullscreen. Use borderless windowed mode for the desktop overlay. Voice remains available in VR. OpenKneeboard integration is on the roadmap.

Can I rely on every answer?+

AI can make mistakes. Indexed manuals provide useful context and source references, but do not guarantee accuracy. Check procedures against your aircraft module’s documentation. DCS Copilote is made for simulation, not real-world flight.
